146 Apple models are in the catalogue, with 56 compatibility groups where an Apple device is the master model. Find which spare parts fit which handset before ordering.

What is recorded for each Apple model

Model name, release date, display size, body dimensions, screen area and battery capacity, together with the compatibility groups the device belongs to across every part category. 47 of the 146 Apple models currently carry compatibility data; the rest are listed with their specifications while groups are added. Fields the source does not carry are shown as “-” rather than estimated.
