Data notes (5)
  • Apple's tech-specs page for iPhone 14 also prints model numbers A2649 and A2632; A2632 belongs to the iPhone 14 Plus because Apple shares a single page across both. Apple's model-identification page (support.apple.com/en-us/108044) is the authority used for the five A-numbers here.
  • Colours: Apple lists six finishes including Yellow, which was added in March 2023. The five TechSpecs records predate that and list five. Apple's list is used; the TechSpecs gap is recorded rather than treated as a contradiction.
  • RAM: TechSpecs reports 6 GB LPDDR4X. 91mobiles states 4 GB. The structured API value is used and the discrepancy is noted; Apple does not publish RAM at all.
  • TechSpecs lists FM Radio under both its absent-features block and its wireless block for this device. The iPhone has no FM radio, so neither value is surfaced.
  • TechSpecs reports the USB port as 'Proprietary, USB 3.1 (3.2 Gen 2)'. Apple states a Lightning connector, and the iPhone 14 Lightning port runs at USB 2.0 speeds. Apple's value is shown; the TechSpecs string is kept in the conflict record.
